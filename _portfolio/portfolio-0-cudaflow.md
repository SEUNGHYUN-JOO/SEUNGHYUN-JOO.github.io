## 🔍 Project Overview

**CuDAFLOW** is a single-precision GPU finite-volume compressible RANS/LES
solver built around an exact **discrete adjoint**. Its distinguishing piece is
**`cuad`**, a Tapenade-style **source-to-source automatic-differentiation
compiler for CUDA** (Clang LibTooling): `cuad` differentiates the actual GPU
residual kernels, so every derivative in the adjoint chain is exact rather than
finite difference.

- **Flow solver (GPU, FP32)** — structured multiblock, overset/chimera, AUSMPW+
  with up to 5th-order MUSCL / eMLP-VC reconstruction, full viscous fluxes,
  Spalart–Allmaras (RANS) and SA-DDES/IDDES/Vreman (LES), implicit red-black /
  line (DADI) smoothing and pseudo-time multigrid, dual-time unsteady.
- **`cuad` — CUDA AD compiler** — forward (tangent) and reverse (adjoint) mode;
  handles arrays, structs, cross-call, `atomicAdd`, ternaries and 2-D array
  access. It differentiates the heavy flux/viscous/turbulence kernels exactly.
- **Exact discrete adjoint** — the stiff RANS adjoint amplifies *any*
  finite-difference term by the condition number, so **all four** amplified
  derivatives — the Jacobian ∂R/∂Q, the objective seed ∂J/∂Q, the mesh
  sensitivity ∂R/∂X and the wall-distance term ∂R/∂wd — are produced by `cuad`
  AD. A 6.85%-accurate FP32 finite-difference Jacobian gave a shape gradient
  **233× too large and the wrong sign**; the full-AD chain recovers the correct
  sign and magnitude.
- **Optimization from Python** — objectives `cd, cl, cf, cp, qw` and design
  variables (FFD control points or the SA field β), driven by pyOptSparse.
  Objective and constraints are plain Python callables of the CFD functionals.
- **PETSc adjoint solve** — the assembled block Jacobian is solved in double
  precision with GMRES + ILU (the only FP64 part; the flow stays FP32).

## 🔧 Engineering Notes

The core finding is that for the stiff RANS discrete adjoint exact AD is not a
luxury: an ill-conditioned adjoint amplifies a 6.85%-accurate finite-difference
Jacobian into a 233× gradient error with the wrong sign. `cuad` makes every
residual derivative exact, and because the amplified terms (∂R/∂Q, ∂R/∂X,
∂R/∂wd) are **objective-independent**, the same machinery serves shape
optimization, Cf/Cp inverse design and Spalart–Allmaras field inversion (FIML)
alike — the choice of objective only enters through the (un-amplified) ∂J/∂Q. A
regression harness checks the AD-Jacobian assembly consistency, the adjoint
convergence, and that the FIML loss actually descends.
